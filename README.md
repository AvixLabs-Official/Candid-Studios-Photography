# Candid Studios London — Self Portrait Studio Notting Hill

Premier self-portrait photography studio website inspired by **[Candid Studios London](https://www.candidstudios.co.uk)** (302 Westbourne Grove, Notting Hill, London W11 2PS).

## 🌟 Overview & Key Features

- **Concept**: Private, photographer-free self-portrait studio where guests hold a wireless remote shutter clicker. Professional studio strobe lighting, interchangeable backdrop sets, and same-day delivery of 250+ high-res images.
- **Top Announcement Bar**: Summer promotional banner with discount code and instant booking link.
- **Interactive Shutter Clicker Simulator Widget**:
  - Live 32-inch studio confidence monitor simulator.
  - Wireless Shutter Button (`PRESS CLICKER 📸`) triggering camera flash animation, shutter click audio sound effect, click counter, and image pose sample transitions.
  - Live Backdrop Swatch Picker (Warm Ivory, Midnight Charcoal, Editorial Crimson, Soft Sage).
- **How It Works Grid**: 4-step explanation of the private self-portrait experience.
- **Curated Backdrop Color Sets**: 4 distinct color environments with instant simulator testing.
- **Transparent Session Pricing**:
  - Solo Self-Portrait (`£75` / 45m)
  - Duo & Couples Experience (`£95` / 45m)
  - Personal Branding & LinkedIn Headshots (`£120` / 60m)
  - Group & Hen Party Celebration (`£180` / 75m)
- **Corporate & Team Headshots**: Team pricing tiers (*The Express*, *Bespoke Studio*, *On-Site Pop-Up*).
- **Candid RetroBooth**: Vintage photobooth walk-in details, physical strip prints, and animated GIFs.
- **Visual Portfolio & Reviews**: Editorial photo grid and verified 5-star customer quotes.
- **Instant Booking API**: Interactive date & time picker form integrated with Express REST API.
